FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Email us and a human replies within a day.

Will it make things up?

No. It only answers from what you've given it. If it isn't sure, it hands the chat to you.

Can I keep using the WhatsApp Business app on my phone?

Yes. Keep replying from your phone, and Captus steps back whenever you jump in.

Is it an official WhatsApp integration?

Yes. Captus uses Meta's official WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger APIs, so your number stays safe.

Which languages does it speak?

English, BM, Chinese, Tamil and 50+ more, including Manglish. It replies in the customer's language.

Is my customers' data safe?

Each business's data is kept separate, and we follow Malaysia's PDPA. Ask us to export or delete it any time.
