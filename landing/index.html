FAQ

Questions, answered

Will it make things up?

No. Captus only answers from the information you give it. If it doesn't know, it says so and hands the chat to you instead of guessing.

Can I take over a conversation?

Yes. Every chat appears in your inbox. Reply yourself and the assistant pauses for that customer until you hand it back.

Do my customers need to install anything?

No. They message your WhatsApp number exactly as they do today.

Is it an official WhatsApp integration?

Yes. Captus uses Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) — no unofficial apps or browser tricks.

How do I update what it knows?

Edit your business info in the dashboard at any time. Changes are used from the very next reply.
