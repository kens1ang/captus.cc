What is Kopi Corner?
Kopi Corner is a neighborhood cafe serving traditional local coffee (kopi), kaya toast, and light bites in a cozy setting. We're a small independent shop, not a chain.

Opening hours.
We're open Tuesday to Sunday, 7:30am to 5pm. We're closed on Mondays for a weekly rest day, and on public holidays.

Menu and prices.
Kopi (traditional coffee with condensed milk): $2.20. Kopi-O (black coffee with sugar): $1.80. Teh (milk tea): $2.20. Kaya toast set (two slices of toast, kaya and butter, two soft-boiled eggs, choice of coffee or tea): $5.50. Sliced bread with kaya only: $2.50. We also do ala-carte soft-boiled eggs at $1.80 for two.

Dine-in and takeaway.
We offer both dine-in (about 20 seats) and takeaway. We don't currently offer delivery ourselves, but we're listed on GrabFood for delivery orders in the surrounding area.

Payment methods.
We accept cash, PayNow, and all major credit/debit cards. We don't accept cheques.

Allergen and dietary notes.
Our kaya toast contains egg, wheat, and dairy. Our kopi/teh drinks are made with condensed and/or evaporated milk (dairy) — we don't have a dairy-free milk alternative at this time. Please let staff know of any allergies before ordering.

WiFi and seating.
We have free WiFi for dine-in customers (ask staff for the password). Seating is first-come first-served — we don't take reservations.

Bulk or catering orders.
For orders of 10 or more kaya toast sets, please message us at least 1 day in advance so we can prepare enough bread and eggs.
